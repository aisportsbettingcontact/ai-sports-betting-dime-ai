== IMPORTANT caveat on mirror independence ==
  cran.wu.ac.at does NOT serve its own copy: it issues a 301 redirect to
  cran.r-project.org (the CRAN master). cloud.r-project.org is a CloudFront
  CDN in front of the same master. The primary and mirror2 indices are
  therefore byte-identical BY CONSTRUCTION (same sha256 above), and their
  agreement is NOT independent corroboration -- it only proves the CDN and
  the redirect both serve the master faithfully.
  To obtain genuine host independence this workstream additionally used the
  designated fallback, ftp.osuosl.org, which is a real rsync mirror on
  separate infrastructure. All independence claims below rest on osuosl.
