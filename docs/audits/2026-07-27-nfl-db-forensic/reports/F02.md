## 2. THE 2012 SNAP-COUNT BOUNDARY — settled

**Where coverage begins.** `snap_count` starts at **season 2013, regular-season week 1**, and
starts complete: **all 32 teams across all 16 week-1 games** are present from the first week. There
is no ramp-up. 2012 has exactly zero rows.

**Does PFR have 2012?** **Yes.** Proven three ways:

| Check | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| A 2012 PFR boxscore carries snap tables | `id="vis_snap_counts"` and `id="home_snap_counts"` both present, with **44 + 46 real player rows** (PHI at NYG, 2012 wk 17) | `cache/f02/probe/wb_pfr_201212300nyg.html` |
| All 267 games of 2012 | **23,820 player-game snap rows parsed from 267 of 267 PFR boxscores** | `cache/f02/pfr/2012*.html.gz`, `2013{01,02}*.html.gz` |
| Volume sanity | 23,820 (2012) vs 23,799 (2013) — the same size. A complete season. | ledger `SEASON_BOUNDARY:2012` |

**Root cause — upstream, and self-contradicting.** nflverse's release *does* ship a 2012 asset:

- `snap_counts_2012.csv` exists — and is **154 bytes: a header row and nothing else**
  (`cache/f02/probe/snap_counts_2012.csv`). `snap_counts_2013.csv` is 2,148,056 bytes.
- nflverse's own manifest `scraped_games_snapcounts.csv` **lists 266 games for 2012** as scraped
  (`cache/f02/probe/scraped_games_snapcounts.csv`). So the project believes it has this data.
- Our extract (`raw/snap_counts.csv`) and this database faithfully reproduce the empty asset.

So the loader is not at fault, and neither is the extract. nflverse publishes an empty 2012 file
while its own manifest says 266 games were scraped, and the boundary at 2013 is an artifact of that
— not a fact about the sport or about PFR.

**Verdict:** `REF_ONLY` on `snap_count` `SEASON_BOUNDARY:2012` — the authority has 23,820 rows we do
not. **This is a significant recoverable gap.** It is recoverable without any new discovery work:
the 267 PFR boxscores are already cached in `cache/f02/pfr/`, and `parse_pfr_snaps()` in
`audit/f02.py` already extracts every field `snap_count` needs, keyed on `pfr_player_id` /
`pfr_game_id` — the table's own primary key.

One incidental discrepancy: the nflverse manifest lists 266 games for 2012, our schedule has 267.
The one it omits is `2012_01_JAX_MIN` (`201209090min`). PFR has that boxscore and it has snap
counts, so the omission is the manifest's, not ours.

## 3. `snap_count` — 23,799 rows, all clean

Every row was compared on **27 fields**: 8 external against PFR (`position`, `offense_snaps`,
`offense_pct`, `defense_snaps`, `defense_pct`, `st_snaps`, `st_pct`, and which side of the box score
the player appears on) and 19 internal/structural.

**Result: 23,799 `MATCH`, 0 `MISMATCH`, 0 `DB_ONLY`, 0 `REF_ONLY`, 0 `UNRESOLVED`.** Not a sample —
every row of the season.

Internal checks that passed on all 23,799 rows:

- every row joins to a real `player` and a real `game`
- `franchise_id` is one of the two franchises in that game, and matches the side of the PFR box
  score the player appears on
- `pfr_game_id`'s embedded date equals `game.gameday` (the venue-local date, not the UTC one)
- `source_week` / `source_game_type` map correctly onto `week` / `playoff_round` / `season_type`
- no impossible values: snaps in [0,150], percentages in [0,1.5]
- **percentages reconcile against team totals.** For each (game, franchise, unit) the unit's snap
  denominator is inferred from the published (snaps, pct) pairs and every row must be explicable by
  a denominator within ±2 of it, to within PFR's whole-percent rounding. All 23,799 pass.
- `franchise_id` equals `franchise_id_upstream` except where a `data_correction` row justifies the
  divergence (none of the 358 D16 corrections fall in 2013)

Three of my own checks were wrong before they were right, and are worth recording so the numbers are
not mistaken for a first-pass result: comparing `pfr_game_id` against UTC rather than venue-local
date produced 4,533 false mismatches; using `max(st_snaps)` as the special-teams denominator
produced 15,319; and a `pfr_player_id` regex that did not allow `.` dropped 426 rows into `DB_ONLY`.
All three were bugs in this audit, not in the database.

## 5. `game` — 534 rows, clean except a systematic NULL

Twelve fields per game against the ESPN scoreboard: `season`, `season_type`, `week` /
`playoff_round`, `home_franchise_id`, `away_franchise_id`, `home_abbr`, `away_abbr`,
`result_status`, `home_score`, `away_score`, `result`, `total`, `overtime`, `time_valid`, `gameday`.
**All 534 games match ESPN on every one of them.** Team identity is compared by ESPN franchise id,
which is immune to retro-renaming.

### 5a. `venue_id` is NULL on every row — 534 × `REF_ONLY`

ESPN publishes a venue id on all 534 competitions and it is already in the cached scoreboards
(e.g. `2012_01_DAL_NYG` → MetLife Stadium, ESPN venue `3839`). The column is NULL.

This is **not confined to my partition**: `venue_id` is NULL on every game from 2010 through 2025 —
**4,363 of 4,648 rows database-wide.** Only 273 of the 285 rows in 2026 are populated. Backfillable
directly from the scoreboard cache. Flagged for the coordinator (§8).

### 5b. Known-good, logged `NOT_COMPARABLE` (contract rule 5)

- `location` — 534 rows. ESPN's `neutralSite` is unpopulated before 2014; it reads `false` even for

- `stadium` — 285 rows. ESPN retro-renames venues (SB XLVII at the "Caesars Superdome", a name that

- `home_abbr` / `away_abbr` — 33 rows. Era-correct label as published vs ESPN's current label, same

- `kickoff_utc` — 6 rows. nflverse stores the scheduled kickoff, ESPN the observed one. Both values
  are in the ledger.

## 6. `roster_season` — 4,257 rows, and an authority that does not work

**The contract's nominated authority for this table cannot rule on these seasons, and I can prove
it.** ESPN's `sports.core.../seasons/{y}/teams/{t}/athletes` endpoint **ignores the season segment
and serves the current roster**:

- its 2012 and 2013 responses are **byte-identical on all 32 of 32 teams**
- only **2 of the 93** athletes it returns for "2012 San Francisco" were active in the NFL in 2012 —
  David Akers, who kicked every game of SF's 2012 season, is not among them

Logged once as `roster_season / AUTHORITY_PROBE` → `NOT_COMPARABLE`, evidence
`cache/f02/roster/roster_2012_25.json.gz`. I did not use it. **No roster row was validated against
it, and no such claim is made anywhere in the ledger.**

**Substitute authority: ESPN box scores.** A player credited with a statistic for a team in season S
is proof of membership; absence proves nothing, since bench and practice-squad players never appear.
Membership is therefore `MATCH` where a box score confirms it and `NOT_COMPARABLE` otherwise —
never `MISMATCH`. `position`, `jersey_number` and `status` have no usable historical source at all
and are `NOT_COMPARABLE` throughout.

**Findings.** 27 rows where `roster_season.full_name` disagrees with `player.display_name` beyond
nickname tolerance — all 27 are legal-name-vs-common-name pairs between the two feeds, none is a
wrong person: Ivan/Ike Taylor, Evander/Ziggy Hood, Cornellius/Tank Carradine, Josh Price-Brent/Josh
Brent, Nickell Robey/Nickell Robey-Coleman, and 22 more, all itemised in the ledger. Benign, but it
means a join on name between these two tables will silently drop them.

17 `REF_ONLY` — a player credited to a team in ESPN box scores with no `roster_season` row for that
team-season. **All 17 trace to the same 12 bad `player.espn_id` values from §4c**, not to missing
roster rows. One root cause, two symptoms.

A characterisation worth passing on, though not a defect of ours: `roster_season` holds 2,031
distinct players for 2012 and 2,032 for 2013, well under the ~2,900 who actually appeared. The
nflverse weekly-roster feed is thin for pre-2016 seasons — 1,063 of 2012's 2,120 rows sit on week 17
alone, with 16–39 rows per week elsewhere.

## 7. `depth_chart` — 74,378 rows, **zero externally validated**

Per the contract: no historical public source for NFL depth charts exists, and ESPN publishes the
current chart only. **Every one of the 74,378 rows is `NOT_COMPARABLE`** with that reason recorded
on the line. **No depth-chart row in this audit was externally validated and none is claimed to be.**

Internal and structural checks — 10 to 12 per row depending on shape, all passing:

- `gsis_id` resolves in `player` (74,378/74,378); identity present (`gsis_id` or `espn_id`)
- `franchise_id` resolves in `team`; `week` / `playoff_round` mutually exclusive; `season_type`
  consistent with whichever is set; `source_shape` B ⇔ `snapshot_ts` present
- `depth_order` ≥ 1 and `source_ordinal` ≥ 1
- `source_week` / `source_game_type` map onto `week` / `playoff_round` correctly
- the charted team actually has a game that season-week

Two structural patterns initially looked like 7,778 defects and are not:

- **3,876 rows with `season_type`, `week` and `playoff_round` all NULL.** Every one is
  `source_game_type='REG', source_week=18, bucket='postseason'` — a post-week-17 chart for a team
  with no next event. The schema CHECK permits NULLs exactly for `bucket IN ('postseason',
  'offseason')`, which holds on all 3,876.
- **3,902 rows charting a team in a week it did not play.** All 64 affected team-weeks are **genuine
  bye weeks** — verified by the team having games both earlier and later that season.

184 natural-key duplicate groups (368 rows) share `(season, source_week, source_game_type,
franchise, player, depth_position, depth_order)`. All are separated by `source_ordinal`, the
documented mechanism for repeated source rows; none collides on `source_ordinal` as well. Logged
`NOT_COMPARABLE` with the count, not silently dropped.

## 8. `game_line`, and what needs a coordinator decision

Every one of the 534 rows was compared against SportsOddsHistory / covers.com, the contract's
nominated authority. The moneyline and juice columns (`away_moneyline`, `home_moneyline`,
`*_spread_odds`, `over_odds`, `under_odds`) are `NOT_COMPARABLE` — SOH publishes neither for these
seasons, and ESPN's odds coverage does not reach 2012–2013.

| | spread_line | total_line |
|---|---:|---:|
| exact match | 228 / 534 (43%) | 345 / 534 (65%) |
| differ by 0.5 | 196 | 152 |
| differ by 1.0 | 56 | 27 |
| differ by ≥ 1.5 | 54 | 10 |
| **disagree on which team was favoured** | **12** | — |

**The two sources genuinely disagree, and spot-checking finds errors on both sides.** All 534
database values reproduce raw nflverse exactly (`cache/a4/nflverse_games.csv.gz`), so nothing was
introduced by our loader. But:

- `2013_12_JAX_HOU` — **nflverse is badly wrong.** It stores `spread_line = 10.5` (Houston favoured
  by 10.5). SOH has Jacksonville favoured by 2 on the road, which matches the actual market: both
  teams were 2–9 and Jacksonville had won three straight. A 12.5-point error, the single worst
  divergence in the partition.
- `2013_16_PIT_GB` and `2013_13_MIA_NYJ` — **SOH is wrong.** SOH makes the home team a 2-point
  favourite in each; nflverse has the road team by 1, which matches what actually happened (Rodgers
  was still injured for the Packers game).

A pattern worth flagging: **53 of the 306 spread mismatches have `|spread_line| == 1.0` on our side,
and 11 of the 12 favourite-disagreements do.** nflverse's `±1` in these seasons behaves like a
low-confidence fallback rather than a real closing number.

**Decision needed:** the contract names covers.com/SOH as the authority for `game_line`, but for
2012–2013 that authority disagrees with the stored value on 57% of spreads and is demonstrably wrong
on at least some of them. Ruling every divergence a database defect would be false. I have logged
them all as `MISMATCH` with both values, as the contract requires, and have not picked a winner. The
coordinator should decide whether `game_line` needs a third source (Pro-Football-Reference publishes
Vegas lines per game and all 534 boxscores are already cached in `cache/f02/pfr/`) before any of
this is called a defect.

**Two further items for the coordinator, both outside my partition but found in it:**

1. **`game.venue_id` is NULL on 4,363 of 4,648 rows database-wide** (§5a). Backfillable from cached
   ESPN scoreboards. Someone owns the whole `game` table and should take this.
2. **`player.espn_id` is wrong or NULL for 12 players** (§4c), affecting 101 player-weeks in my two
   seasons alone and certainly more elsewhere. The correct ids are in the table in §4c.

**`data_correction`:** all 624 corrections were scanned and resolved to a season.
**Zero target a 2012 or 2013 row** — nothing to re-verify in my partition. The 91 D11 and 34 D15
`game` corrections, the 358 D16 `snap_count` corrections and the 83 D17/D19 `player_game_stats`
corrections all land in other seasons.
