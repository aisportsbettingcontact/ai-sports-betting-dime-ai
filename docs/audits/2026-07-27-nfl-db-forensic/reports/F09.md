## 2. The `gameday` timezone adjudication

### The disagreement

- `INTEGRATION.md` D11: derive `gameday` "in **the venue's local zone**". `schema.sql` agrees
  (`gameday TEXT, -- venue-local calendar date (D11)`).
- `CONTEXT.md`: "`kickoff_date` is derived in **Pacific** time." The correction actually applied
  says so explicitly — its `source` column reads *"kickoff_utc converted to America/Los_Angeles
  per CONTEXT.md's kickoff-datetime convention"*.

### The ruling: **both documents are wrong, and Pacific — the rule that shipped — is the worse of the two**

The contract makes ESPN the authority for `game`. Rather than theorise about which zone ESPN uses,
I asked ESPN directly: its date-bucketed scoreboard (`scoreboard?dates=YYYYMMDD`) *is* a statement
of the local game date. I fetched every 2026 gameday ±1 (138 dates, cached) and recorded which
bucket each of the 285 events lands in. Scoring the three candidate rules against that:

| Rule | Reproduces ESPN's bucket | Fails on |
|---|---:|---|
| **Eastern** (`America/New_York`) | **285 / 285** | — |
| venue-local (`INTEGRATION.md`, `schema.sql`) | 274 / 285 | 11 rows |
| **Pacific (implemented, and what D11 wrote)** | **249 / 285** | **36 rows** |

Neither documented rule survives. Eastern is the only one that reproduces the authority on every
row, and it is not the one anybody wrote down.

### Why Pacific fails — and this is the live defect

36 of the 285 rows carry `time_valid = 0`. Every one of them has `kickoff_utc = …T05:00:00Z`,
which is **midnight US Eastern on the game date** — ESPN's encoding for *"date known, kickoff time
not yet set."* ESPN publishes `timeValid: false` on exactly these 36 and the DB mirrors it
correctly. It is a date marker, not an instant.

Converting a midnight-ET date marker into *any* zone west of Eastern lands on the previous day.
So Pacific derivation silently rolls all 36 back 24 hours:

| ESPN bucket (authority) | Stored `gameday` | Rows | What they are |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 2026-12-27 | 2026-12-26 | 4 | Week 16 flex slots |
| 2027-01-03 | 2027-01-02 | 4 | Week 17 flex slots |
| 2027-01-10 | 2027-01-09 | 16 | Week 18 — the whole final Sunday |
| 2027-01-16 / -17 / -18 | one day earlier | 6 | Wild Card |
| 2027-01-23 / -24 | one day earlier | 4 | Divisional |
| 2027-01-31 | 2027-01-30 | 2 | Conference Championships |

The Super Bowl is unaffected (its kickoff is a real instant, `2027-02-14T23:30:00Z`).

**D11 made these 36 rows worse.** Before the correction `gameday` was the UTC date — which for a
midnight-ET marker is *the correct date*. D11 rewrote 91 rows from UTC-date to Pacific-date: 55 of
those were genuine fixes (late Sunday/Monday kickoffs whose UTC date really was a day ahead), and
36 were regressions. A wrong correction now carries an authoritative-looking provenance record,
which is precisely the failure mode this audit was told to hunt for.

Venue-local fails on 11 rows for the same reason plus one extra: 10 of the 36 markers sit at
venues west of Eastern, and `2026_01_SF_LAR` (Melbourne Cricket Ground, `2026-09-11T00:35:00Z`)
is a real instant where venue-local says 2026-09-11 while ESPN files it under **2026-09-10**
(evidence: `cache/f09/byday/sb_20260910.json` contains event 401872657; `sb_20260911.json` is
empty). That single row is the case the task flagged, and it settles against `INTEGRATION.md`.

### Recommendation to the coordinator

1. Derive `gameday` from `kickoff_utc` in **`America/New_York`**, not Pacific and not venue-local.
   It reproduces ESPN on 285/285 and is the only rule that survives the `time_valid = 0` markers.
   For every real kickoff in this database Eastern and Pacific agree anyway — the NFL never starts
   a game between 00:00 and 03:00 ET — so the change costs nothing and fixes 36 rows.
2. Amend `INTEGRATION.md` D11 and the `schema.sql` comment: `gameday` is not the venue's local
   date, and pretending it is breaks the Melbourne game.
3. Add a build gate: `time_valid = 0` implies `gameday = substr(kickoff_utc, 1, 10)`. A date
   marker must never be run through a timezone conversion.
4. Re-stamp the 36 D11 corrections. As applied they are wrong; leaving them makes the audit trail
   assert a false fact with a citation attached.

I also asserted that **no** 2026 row carries a value it cannot have: `away_score`, `home_score`,

all 285. Zero `DB_ONLY`. Nothing was fabricated to fill an unplayed season.

`location` on the 12 placeholders is NULL where ESPN sends `neutralSite: false`. That is ESPN's
struct default on a row with no venue and no teams, not a ruling — logged `NOT_COMPARABLE`.

### 4.5 Field-level disagreements — itemised

**435 `college` MISMATCH.** These collapse to **112 distinct `(ESPN college id → nflverse college)`

not per-row corruption. Two causes, both external:

  `cache/f09/derived/college_id_fanout.json`.

`draft_pick` is otherwise **`NOT_COMPARABLE`** wherever ESPN's `draft.pick` `$ref` shows its
