## 1. Verdict

The 2014 and 2015 partitions are sound. Across 159,157 audited rows there are **7 rows carrying
a genuine content disagreement with an authority**, and every one of them is a divergence
*between the upstream sources themselves*, not a corruption introduced by this database.

The two headline questions both resolve cleanly:

- **The Super Bowl snap transposition (D16) is fixed and independently re-verified.** All 177
  corrected rows in my seasons were checked one by one against Pro-Football-Reference — the
  contract authority — and PFR places every one of them in the corrected franchise's snap table.
- **The transposition does not extend beyond the two Super Bowls.** All 534 games in both
  seasons were scanned with two independent detectors. Zero further transpositions: 32,803/32,803
  ESPN-checkable snap rows on the correct franchise, 11,916/11,916 PFR-checkable rows on the correct
  franchise, all 22 postseason games clean, all 512 regular-season games clean.

One systematic gap is worth escalating: **`game.venue_id` is NULL for all 534 rows** — and, on
inspection, for all 4,363 rows in seasons 2010-2025 database-wide — while ESPN publishes a venue
id for every one of them.

  week/playoff_round exclusivity holds. **All 35,302 pass. That is internal consistency, not
  external confirmation, and it is not represented as external anywhere in the ledger.**

## 13. For the coordinator

1. **`game.venue_id` is NULL for 4,363 of 4,648 rows database-wide** while ESPN publishes a venue

2. **`game_line`'s external agreement is inherently low and that is expected.** nflverse,
   covers.com and ESPN carry three different market snapshots and agree with each other no better

6. **2014's depth charts are ~4,500 rows short of every neighbouring season, upstream.** (§10)
7. **74% of `snap_count` in my partition is internally validated only.** Full PFR coverage is
