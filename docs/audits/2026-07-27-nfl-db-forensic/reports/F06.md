#### F06-I — `roster_season` semantics, now established (closes part of D24)

The table is **not** a weekly roster. It holds one row per player per (season, `source_game_type`),
and `source_week` records the **last** week that player appears in the weekly feed — in 2020,
1,494 of the 1,893 regular-season rows sit on week 17, with the other 399 spread across weeks 1–16
(those are the players who left). A player who changed teams mid-season therefore appears **only
under his final team**. Any join that treats `(season, franchise_id, source_week)` as "who was on
this roster in week *w*" is wrong. This is what produces the 1,710 depth-chart rows in §2.7.

#### The 1,710 `on_roster_that_season` misses are a `roster_season` artefact, not a `depth_chart` defect

1,710 rows (2020: 757, 2021: 953) place a player on a franchise's depth chart while
`roster_season` has no row for that player at that franchise that season. They reduce to **257
distinct (player, season, franchise) combinations, and every single one of those players is on
*some* team's `roster_season` in that season — zero are phantom players.** Spot-checking the
combinations shows the mechanism directly: Tramon Williams is on franchise 33's chart in weeks
11–16 of 2020 while `roster_season` records him only at franchise 9; Takk McKinley is on franchise
1's chart in weeks 1–7 while `roster_season` records him only at franchise 13. These are
mid-season movers, and F06-I explains why they cannot match. **The depth-chart rows are correct;
the cross-table check is defeated by `roster_season`'s last-team-only semantics.**

## 3. The 17-game season and the week-18 collision

**Verified, and the encoding is sound.**

- Every one of the 32 franchises plays exactly **16** regular-season games in 2020 and exactly
  **17** in 2021. (`SELECT season, n, COUNT(*) FROM (SELECT season, franchise_id, COUNT(*) n FROM
  team_game WHERE season IN (2020,2021) AND season_type='REG' GROUP BY 1,2) GROUP BY 1,2` →
  `2020|16|32`, `2021|17|32`.)
- Totals reconcile: 2020 = 256 REG + 13 POST = 269; 2021 = 272 REG + 13 POST = 285. Both
  postseasons have the expanded 14-team bracket (6 WC + 4 DIV + 2 CON + 1 SB).
- 2021's week 18 is stored as `season_type='REG', week=18, playoff_round=NULL` on 16 games. No
  postseason row in either season carries a `week` at all — the schema's mutual-exclusion CHECK
  makes the old week-18 ambiguity unrepresentable, and no row in this partition attempts it.

**The collision is real, but it lives in `depth_chart`, and the loader handles it.** nflverse keeps
publishing a `game_type='REG'` chart one week past the real regular season, and its continuous
week counter shifted with the 17-game season:

| season | trailing REG chart | wild-card chart | how the DB stores the trailing chart |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2020 | `REG` / `source_week=18` (1,838 rows) | `WC` / `source_week=18` (733 rows) | `season_type`, `week`, `playoff_round` all NULL, `bucket='postseason'` |
| 2021 | `REG` / `source_week=19` (1,754 rows) | `WC` / `source_week=19` (679 rows) | same |

In 2020, `REG/18` and `WC/18` share a source week number — exactly the collision to watch for. The
loader disambiguates on `source_game_type`, declines to mint a regular-season week 18 for a season
that had 17, and buckets those rows as postseason. All 73,655 rows pass the `week_encoding` check.
2021's real week 18 is distinct from the trailing `REG/19` chart and from `WC/19`.

## 4. COVID rescheduling and rest days — the headline correction to the brief

### 4.1 The 2020 season was rescheduled far more than 2021, and its rest days are already right

The brief expected 2020 to be the densest season for the D15 rest-day defect class. **It is not.
2020 needs zero rest corrections, and that is verified against an external source, not just
internally.**

Three independent recomputations agree:

1. **Stored vs recomputed from `game.gameday`** — 0 disagreements across all 1,108 team-slots.
2. **Stored vs recomputed from ESPN's own kickoff timestamps** (554 cached ESPN summaries,
   converted to US/Pacific and differenced per franchise) — **0 disagreements across all 1,108
   team-slots.** `game.gameday` also equals ESPN's Pacific date on all 554 rows.
3. **nflverse upstream vs ESPN-derived actual rest** — **20 disagreements, all 20 in 2021, none in
   2020.** Those 20 are exactly the 20 D15 corrections recorded in `data_correction` for this
   partition, value for value.

So: **all 20 D15 corrections in my partition are re-verified correct against ESPN, and a
full sweep of every 2020 game found no further rest-day error.** The reason 2020 escapes is
visible in the data — nflverse's rest column already carries the *irregular* values that
rescheduling produced:

| rescheduled game | played | away rest (stored / nflverse) | home rest (stored / nflverse) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `2020_05_BUF_TEN` | Tue 13 Oct | 9 / 9 | **16 / 16** |
| `2020_12_BAL_PIT` | Wed 2 Dec | 10 / 10 | 10 / 10 |
| `2020_13_DAL_BAL` | Tue 8 Dec | **12 / 12** | 6 / 6 |
| `2020_13_WAS_PIT` | Mon 7 Dec | 11 / 11 | **5 / 5** |
| `2020_04_NE_KC` | Mon 5 Oct | 8 / 8 | 7 / 7 |
| `2020_16_BUF_NE` | Mon 28 Dec | 9 / 9 | 8 / 8 |

2020's postponements were absorbed into the published schedule before nflverse froze the season, so
its `rest` was computed against the played date. December 2021's postponements were not. (The
absorption is the observable fact; the freezing sequence is my inference from it.)

### 4.2 What the rescheduling actually looks like in this partition

- **Games outside the Thursday/Saturday/Sunday/Monday window** — 2020: `2020_05_BUF_TEN` (Tue),
  `2020_12_BAL_PIT` (Wed), `2020_13_DAL_BAL` (Tue), plus the scheduled Christmas Friday
  `2020_16_MIN_NO`. 2021: `2021_15_SEA_LA` and `2021_15_WAS_PHI` (both Tue).
- **Monday games** — 20 in 2020 against 18 in 2021 and a scheduled norm of 17.
- **Weeks whose games span more than four calendar days** — 2020 week 5 (5 days), 2020 week 12
  (6 days), 2021 week 15 (5 days).
- **The week-4 postponement is encoded as a week move, not a ghost row.** Pittsburgh and Tennessee
  have no week-4 game at all (week 4 holds 15 games); their meeting is stored once as
  `2020_07_PIT_TEN` in week 7. No duplicate, no orphan, and both franchises still total 16.
- **Crowds** — ESPN reports **attendance 0 for 153 of the 269 2020 games (56.9%)**, with a median
  non-zero crowd of 11,133 and a maximum of 31,700. In 2021, zero games had zero attendance and
  the median crowd was 67,808. That is the COVID season in one line, sourced from ESPN.

`game.temp` / `game.wind` were not comparable: ESPN publishes no weather block on these summaries.

Four observations the owner should have before deciding:

1. **Ten of ten quotes put the away price between −148 and −159.** Not one is near −124.
2. **The home price is not in dispute.** The DB's `+134` sits inside the ESPN range [+126, +135];
   the spread `−3.0` equals the ESPN mode exactly; the total `48.0` equals the ESPN mode exactly,

## 7. Authority reachability — two findings other agents need

### 7.1 Pro-Football-Reference is unreachable (403)

See §2.5. This is not a rate-limit problem that patience solves — it is a Cloudflare challenge
returned on the first request from three different clients. `lib/corrections.py` already notes
"PFR 403s" for the Jalen Davis investigation, so this is now confirmed twice, independently.
**Any conclusion in this audit programme that depends on PFR is currently unreachable, and the
snap-count table has no other authority.** If PFR coverage is required, it needs a route this
environment does not have.

### 7.2 ESPN's historical season-roster endpoint is wrong and must not be used

`sports.core.api.espn.com/.../seasons/{y}/teams/{id}/athletes` was fetched for all 64 team-seasons
in this partition (all HTTP 200, all cached). It is **not** the roster of that team in that season:

> For 2020 Atlanta it returns 92 athletes. **Only 3 of them are among the 56 athletes who appear in
> Atlanta's own 2020 ESPN box scores.** Across the partition, only 318 of 5,916 of our roster rows'
> ESPN ids appear in the corresponding document.

Using it as a membership authority would have produced **5,598 false MISMATCHes** — 93% of the
table — which is how it presented on the first pass. The endpoint is retained in the cache as the
evidence for that determination and is not used to rule on any row. Membership is instead confirmed
positively from ESPN box scores, which can only confirm, never refute; a miss is NOT_COMPARABLE.
