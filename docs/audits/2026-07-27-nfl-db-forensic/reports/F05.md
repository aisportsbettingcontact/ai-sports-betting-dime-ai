## 3. Every MISMATCH, REF_ONLY and UNRESOLVED, itemised

### F05-1 — `game.referee` misspelled (MISMATCH ×1) — **real defect**

| game_id | db_value | ESPN | evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `2018_11_MIN_CHI` | `John Perry` | `John Parry` | `cache/f05/summary_401030903.json.gz` |

ESPN's officials block for event 401030903 names **John Parry** as Referee. The database itself
spells it `John Parry` on 136 other games and `John Perry` on only two — this one and
`2017_04_OAK_DEN` (event 400951744), which is outside my partition and belongs to whoever owns
2017. The same crew sheet lists a back judge named *Perry* Paganelli, the likely contamination.

**Recommend: correct both rows to `John Parry`.**

### F05-2 — `game.venue_id` is NULL on every completed game (REF_ONLY ×534) — **table-wide gap**

All 534 partition rows have `venue_id IS NULL` while ESPN publishes a venue id on every summary.
This is not a 2018/2019 artifact: `venue_id` is NULL on **4,363 of 4,648 rows**, i.e. every
completed game from 2010 through 2025, and populated only on 273 of the 285 unplayed 2026 rows.
The loader evidently never wrote it for historical seasons.

Logged `REF_ONLY` (authority has it, we do not), not `MISMATCH` — the database asserts nothing.
`game.stadium` and `stadium_id` are populated, so this is a lost join key rather than lost
information. Cross-partition; flagging for the coordinator.

### F05-3 — `2018_02_LAC_BUF`: one completed pass missing (MISMATCH ×5) — **real defect**

### F05-6 — Zach Ertz is absent from ESPN (UNRESOLVED ×34) — **defect in the authority, not the database**

All 34 of Zach Ertz's 2018–2019 rows are `UNRESOLVED`: he appears in **none** of his 34 ESPN box
scores, under any id or any name. Corroboration that the database is right and ESPN is wrong:

1. `raw/player_stats.csv` carries every line.
2. In `2018_01_ATL_PHI`, ESPN's five listed Eagles receivers match the database *exactly*
   (Agholor 8/33, Sproles 4/22, Foles 1/15, Carter 1/10, Goedert 1/4) and sum to 84 receiving
   yards; the database has one more receiver — Ertz, 5/48 — and 84 + 48 = 132, the database's
   team total. ESPN's list is short by precisely his line.
3. `athletes/15835` returns **HTTP 404** from both the core and site APIs
   (agent S2 hit the same 404 earlier today: `cache/s2/ertz_core_15835.json`).
4. ESPN's search returns Ertz only under a GUID, with no numeric athlete id
   (`cache/f05/espn_search_zach_ertz.json`).

Kept `UNRESOLVED`, never downgraded to `MATCH`: the named authority cannot rule.

### F05-8 — 12 `snap_count` ST percentages do not reconcile (UNRESOLVED ×12) — **upstream artifact**

Two team-games: `2019_01_DET_ARI` (DET, 6 rows) and `2019_12_JAX_TEN` (JAX, 6 rows). No single
integer special-teams total reproduces all the published percentages — the best fit covers 20 of

### F05-9 — 42 identity-less upstream rows (REF_ONLY ×42) — **correctly excluded**

`raw/player_stats.csv` carries 34,776 rows for 2018–2019 against the database's 34,734. All 42
extra rows have an **empty `player_id` and empty name** — one aggregate row per week (21 weeks ×
2 seasons). They cannot become `player_game_stats` rows because `gsis_id` references
`player(gsis_id)`. Logged `REF_ONLY` under synthetic keys (`upstream_ghost/…`) and excluded from
the partition count, since they are not partition rows.

One of them is independently interesting: the week-9 2018 row is labelled team **`LV`**, a
franchise label that did not exist until 2020 — evidence that these synthetic rows are generated
against a current team dimension, and a reminder of why §4 exists.

statistic block and refusing to name-match when a name is ambiguous within a game. The database
was right both times.

## 5. `roster_season`: the contract's authority does not exist

ESPN publishes **no historical roster**. `seasons/{y}/teams/{id}/athletes` returns ~92 ids per
team and covers only **149 of this partition's 5,845 players** — under 3%. It also names Mike
Vrabel as New England's 2018 coach, which is a 2025 fact. The `types/2/…` variant returns an
empty list. All 64 responses are cached (`cache/f05/roster_{season}_{fid}.json.gz`) so the claim
is checkable, but the endpoint is not treated as able to rule.

Substituted a real one: **a player who recorded a box-score line for a franchise that season was
demonstrably on it.** That confirms 3,429 of 6,256 rows (55%) from the 534 cached summaries. The
remaining 2,825 are `NOT_COMPARABLE` with the reason stated — mostly backups and inactives who
never recorded a stat. Two more are `NOT_COMPARABLE` for the known upstream rows carrying no
`gsis_id` (N9).

Separately, **every one of the 6,256 rows reconciles field-for-field against
`raw/rosters.csv`** — position, depth-chart position, jersey, status, name, years of experience.
Zero mismatches, zero rows missing upstream. The table's integrity is established; only external
membership confirmation is partial.

`depth_chart` is similar but worse and the contract already says so: no historical public source
exists, so all 72,868 rows carry an `external_authority` line with `NOT_COMPARABLE`. Internally
all 72,868 pass structural checks, and **all 72,868 match `raw/depth_charts.csv` exactly** — the
raw file has precisely 72,868 rows for these seasons, so transport is 1:1 with nothing added or
lost.

## 6. Documented deviations from the letter of the contract

Two, both stated rather than hidden:

1. **Grouped `NOT_COMPARABLE` for two uniform column sets.** `player_game_stats`'s seven modelled
   columns (`passing_epa`, `rushing_epa`, `receiving_epa`, `target_share`, `air_yards_share`,
   `fantasy_points`, `fantasy_points_ppr`) share one reason — no box score publishes them — so
   each row gets one record naming all seven rather than seven identical records. This is the
   same folding logic the contract applies to `MATCH`, and it saved ~208,000 lines on an already
   128 MB ledger. Every row still carries its verdict and reason. Their **transport is separately
   verified**: all seven are compared against `raw/player_stats.csv` on every row and folded into
   the row-level counts, so they are not unchecked.
2. **Evidence paths are relative to `scripts/data/nfl-db/`**, matching the contract's own example
   (`cache/s03/sum_400554689.json`). Resolve them from that directory. All 1,742 verified present.
