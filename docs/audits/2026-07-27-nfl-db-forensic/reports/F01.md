## 1. Verdict

The 2010–2011 partition is **sound**. Every game row reconciles with ESPN on every field ESPN can
rule on, every derived `team_game` row recomputes exactly, and 92% of the 34,988 player stat rows
match the ESPN box score field for field. There are no fabrications: **zero `REF_ONLY` rows and only
2 `DB_ONLY` rows** in the entire partition, and both of those are trick plays ESPN simply did not
tabulate.

Three things need a coordinator decision, all detailed in §6:

1. **Defect D17 was applied incompletely.** It corrected nine counting columns but left
   `sacks_suffered`, `target_share`, `fantasy_points` and `fantasy_points_ppr` still computed from
   the duplicated plays, in both affected games. Evidenced arithmetically and against ESPN.
2. **`player.espn_id` is wrong or missing for 13 players**, affecting 116 rows in my partition. Out
   of my partition (the `player` table) but it blocks stat verification, so I resolved around it and
   logged every instance.
3. **`snap_count` is missing the entire 2012 season.** Out of my partition, found while proving my
   own snap boundary. Upstream publishes 2012; the database starts at 2013.

As predicted, 2010–2011 produces more `NOT_COMPARABLE` than later years will. That is honest: ESPN's
`neutralSite` is dead before 2014, it publishes no historical weather, its venue attributes are
current-state, and its historical roster endpoints return today's roster. I did not relax any
standard to manufacture a match.

## 6. For the coordinator

### 6.1 D17 was applied incompletely — four columns still carry the duplicated plays

D17 corrected nine columns (`targets`, `completions`, `attempts`, `passing_yards`, `receptions`,
`receiving_yards`, `carries`, `rushing_yards`, `rushing_tds`). Four more columns were computed from
the same duplicated play-by-play and were **not** corrected.

**(a) `sacks_suffered` — a phantom sack survives in each game.**

| row | db | ESPN box score | ESPN team total |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `2011_10_DET_CHI\|00-0026498` (Stafford) | 3 | **2** | DET offense 2-16 |
| `2011_13_DET_NO\|00-0026498` (Stafford) | 4 | **3** | DET offense 3-29 |

Evidence: `cache/a5/summary_311113003.json.gz`, `cache/a5/summary_311204018.json.gz`.

**(b) `target_share` — 25 rows still divide by the uncorrected team target total.** This is provable
arithmetic, not inference. `2011_10_DET_CHI|00-0021182` stores 0.0308 = 2/65, the pre-correction
Detroit target total; the corrected total is 60 and 2/60 = 0.0333.
`2011_13_DET_NO|00-0021182` stores 0.1569 = 8/51 — both numerator *and* denominator pre-correction —
where the corrected values give 7/44 = 0.1591. All 25 rows itemised in the ledger.

**(c) `fantasy_points` and `fantasy_points_ppr` — 12 rows cannot be reconciled with their own row.**
Every term this table does not store (2-point conversion +2, lost fumble −2, ST/defensive TD +6)
moves fantasy points by a **multiple of 2**. I recomputed fantasy points from the stored columns for
all 34,988 rows in the partition and took the residual. **Exactly 12 rows have a residual that is not
a multiple of 2 — and all 12 are in the two D17 games** (10 in `2011_13_DET_NO`, 2 in
`2011_10_DET_CHI`). Worst cases: Mark Ingram stored 25.2 vs 11.4 recomputed (residual 13.8), Kevin
Smith 23.1 vs 14.0 (9.1), Robert Meachem 23.3 vs 18.7 (4.6). No combination of unstored terms can
produce a residual of 13.8 or 0.36; the stored values were computed from different yardage than the
row now holds.

That same test is a whole-partition detector, and it is clean everywhere else — which is itself the
strongest available evidence that no third game carries this defect.

**(d) The EPA columns** (`passing_epa`, `rushing_epa`, `receiving_epa`) are non-NULL in both games and
no D17 correction touches them. They were derived from the duplicated play-by-play. I cannot verify
EPA values against any public source, so I log them NOT_COMPARABLE — but they should be assumed
contaminated in these two games.

**Recommendation:** re-derive `sacks_suffered`, `target_share`, `air_yards_share`, `fantasy_points`,
`fantasy_points_ppr` and the three EPA columns for `2011_10_DET_CHI` and `2011_13_DET_NO`, and record
the additional corrections under D17.

### 6.2 How far the D17 duplication signature extends in 2010–2011: nowhere else

This was the assignment's open question. Three independent sweeps, all 534 games, all 34,988 rows:

1. **Full ESPN box-score comparison, field by field.** 57 field disagreements over 40 player-games,
   listed in §4.2b. Every one is ±1 to ±3 units, and most reconcile exactly at team level — the
   opposite of the D17 signature (a whole drive duplicated, inflating several players at once by
   large margins).
2. **Multi-category inflation scan.** Six games contain a player whose line exceeds ESPN in two or
   more categories at once: `2010_01_IND_HOU`, `2010_01_SD_KC`, `2010_04_HOU_OAK`, `2010_04_NE_MIA`,
   `2010_05_STL_DET`, `2010_06_CLE_PIT`. In every case it is one player, offset by a teammate, with
   the team total intact. None resembles D17.
3. **Fantasy-point residual test** (§6.1c) — flags the two known games and nothing else.

**Conclusion: the D17 play-duplication defect is confined to `2011_10_DET_CHI` and `2011_13_DET_NO`
within seasons 2010 and 2011.** Neither of those games appears in sweep 1 or 2 any more, because the
counting-stat corrections worked.

## 8. Disclosed deviation: how NOT_COMPARABLE is recorded

The contract requires a per-field record for every non-MATCH outcome. I followed that for every
comparison that was actually attempted. For **`depth_chart`, where no authority exists at all**, and
for `player_game_stats.position` / `position_group`, non-comparability is a property of the *table*,
not of any row. Emitting one line per (row, field) would have restated a single fact 530,973 times
and produced a 408 MB ledger. Those field **names are recorded individually on each row's
row-level record**, in `not_comparable_fields`, alongside `fields_not_comparable` — nothing is lost
and the data is machine-extractable per row — plus one table-level declaration record
(`row_key: "*"`) stating the rule. Per-field lines are still written for every internal check that
could fail, and for every row-specific non-comparability (non-NULL EPA, non-zero `air_yards_share`,
kickoff deltas, venue renames, and so on).

Net effect: 155,561 lines / 96 MB instead of 806,095 lines / 408 MB, with the same per-row
information. Flagging it explicitly so the supervisors can reject it if they disagree.
