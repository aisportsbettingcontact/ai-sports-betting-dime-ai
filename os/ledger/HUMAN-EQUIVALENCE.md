# Human equivalence — the D10 comparison basis

**Status:** BLOCKED — needs Prez input
**observe_by:** 2026-08-19

D10's sixth ledger question is *"What human organization would the same result have required?"* It
is the question that turns a token bill into a capital-allocation argument, and it is the one most
prone to a flattering guess.

**It is deliberately unanswered.** `os/ledger/LEDGER.md` reports it as NOT ANSWERED rather than
estimating it, because an invented number here is precisely the "token waste mistaken for
token-maxing" failure D15 #14 names.

## What it needs from Prez

1. **A stated plan fee** — the actual monthly subscription cost, so replacement cost can be compared
   against real outlay rather than against list price.
2. **Ratified assumption ranges** for a costed reconstruction:
   - fully-loaded cost of a senior engineer in Dime's market
   - fully-loaded cost of a data/ML engineer
   - how many of each the measured output would conventionally require
   - whether to include recruiting, onboarding, management and coordination burden (D10 says the
     comparison *should* include them)

## Why it is not guessed

The honest comparison is not "model bill versus zero". It is model bill versus **the full cost of
the human organization otherwise required** — salary, benefits, recruiting, onboarding, management,
communication, and the coordination burden of a larger group. Every one of those is a number only
Prez can supply or ratify.

Until then this file stays open, and the clock will report it.
